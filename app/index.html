Information

  • About the project period
    • Three weeks of supervised research in P3 (January) and P6 (June), 5 ECTS.
    • Students list a top five from the catalogue; the committee allocates.
    • A project runs only if it reaches its minimum number of students.
  • How to offer a project
    • One page of description plus the lab and group-size details, through the form.
    • The committee reviews it, assigns a code and publishes the catalogue.
    • Meet the group in the period before; budget table a month ahead; grades within five days after.
